💡 Tips for Staying in Control

Follow these simple guidelines to keep betting enjoyable and under control:

  • Set a budget before you start and stick to it — treat it like any other entertainment spend.
  • Only gamble with money you can afford to lose — never borrow money or use funds meant for essentials.
  • Set time limits for your sessions and take regular breaks.
  • Never chase losses — they're part of the game. Walk away and come back another day.
  • Balance gambling with other hobbies, family, and social time.
  • Avoid gambling when upset, stressed, or under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
  • Review your activity regularly in the dashboard and bet history.
