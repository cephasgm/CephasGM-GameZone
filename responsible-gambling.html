Responsible Gambling

Play safe. Stay in control.

Our Commitment

At CephasGM GameZone, we want every player to enjoy betting as a form of entertainment. We are committed to providing a safe, fair, and transparent environment. We encourage all our users to gamble responsibly and to be aware of the risks associated with gambling.

Tips for Staying in Control

  • Set a budget before you start and stick to it.
  • Only gamble with money you can afford to lose.
  • Set time limits for your gambling sessions.
  • Never chase losses – they are part of the game.
  • Balance gambling with other hobbies and activities.
  • Avoid gambling when you are upset, stressed, or under the influence of alcohol.
  • Take regular breaks and review your activity.

Need Help?

If you feel that gambling is becoming a problem for you or someone you know, please seek help. The following organisations provide free, confidential support:

  • Gamblers Anonymous – www.gamblersanonymous.org
  • BeGambleAware – www.begambleaware.org
  • GamCare – www.gamcare.org.uk
  • National Council on Problem Gambling – www.ncpgambling.org
