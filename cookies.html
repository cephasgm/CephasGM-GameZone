🍪 Cookie Policy

How we use cookies and similar technologies on CephasGM GameZone — and how you can control them.

ℹ️
Quick summary: We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and improve our platform. Essential cookies are required for the site to work. You can control non-essential cookies at any time.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help us recognise your device, remember your preferences, and improve your overall experience.

Cookies do not harm your device and are essential for many online services, including keeping you logged in and remembering your bet slip selections.

2 How We Use Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core functionality such as logging in, account security, and navigation.
  • Performance Cookies: Help us understand how you use the site (pages visited, time spent) so we can improve performance.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences like language, odds display format, and bet slip selections.
  • Advertising Cookies: Deliver relevant promotions and measure campaign effectiveness. These may be set by third-party ad networks.

4 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by third-party services that appear on our pages. We do not control these cookies. They are used for analytics, social media integration, and advertising.

Please refer to the respective third-party privacy policies for more details on how they collect and use your data.

5 Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the Platform and prevent you from placing bets or accessing certain features.

To opt out of Google Analytics, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6 Consent

When you first visit our site, a cookie banner will appear asking for your consent to non-essential cookies. You can accept all cookies, reject non-essential ones, or customise your preferences.

Your consent can be withdrawn at any time by clearing your browser cookies or changing your preferences via the banner.

7 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy occasionally to reflect changes in technology or legislation. We will notify you of significant changes via a notice on our website. Continued use of the Platform after changes indicates acceptance.

8 Contact Us

If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@cephasgm.org.

Need more information?

Our team responds to privacy and cookie questions within 24 hours.
